SOP-44: Corrosion Inspection Protocol

Subject: Pressure Vessel Corrosion Limits
Date: 2024-11-15
Author: Engineering Department

1. Scope
This SOP defines the corrosion inspection limits for pressure vessels in the facility.

2. Corrosion Limits
The maximum allowable corrosion depth for pressure vessels is 5mm. Any vessel showing corrosion exceeding 5mm must be immediately taken out of service and reported to the safety officer.

3. Inspection Frequency
All pressure vessels must be inspected quarterly using ultrasonic thickness measurement.

4. Documentation
All inspection results must be recorded in the maintenance log and reported to the engineering manager.

Reference: ASME Section VIII, Division 1
